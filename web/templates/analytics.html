Event sessions and returns

US cash equities use America/New_York. BMO maps to the report trading session; AMC maps to the next trading session. Weekends, exchange holidays, and documented exceptional closures are excluded. Unsupported or unknown timing produces N/A.

Opening Gap = event daily open / previous regular session close − 1. Event-day return = event daily close / previous session close − 1. Forward returns start at event-day close and use 1, 2, 5, 10, 21, or 63 trading sessions. Missing bars do not shorten a horizon. Same-day daily bars are withheld until the next local date.

Daily open and close use the same split-adjusted OHLC series. Historical intraday observations remain stored but are optional and are not required by these analytics.

Statistics and sources

Returns are stored as fractions, displayed as percentages. Provider surprises are stored as percentage points. Win rate counts strictly positive returns. Null and non-finite values are excluded; zero returns remain in sample counts. Standard deviation uses n − 1 and needs at least two observations.

Beat/miss groups require nonzero EPS surprise only; large groups use strictly greater than +10 or less than −10 percentage points. Gap bucket boundaries: >5%, [2%,5%], (−2%,2%), [−5%,−2%], <−5%. A zero gap belongs to the central bucket.

SEC fundamentals use directly reported 75–105-day periods; no annual/YTD totals are relabeled as quarters. Fiscal labels remain N/A if they cannot be reliably assigned. Revenue and EPS retain separate source labels. No forecast is drawn without a reliable free estimate.

Yahoo is unofficial and can restrict access. Missing data, including historical revenue estimates and intraday history, remains N/A. The application does not assert that an empty calendar means no earnings.
